Etali's Constitution

The goal is to enjoy the strongest decks within our evolving restrictions.

Current Restrictions

  • No Game Changers
  • No 2 Card Infinites
  • No Free Interaction No surprises from tapped out players
    • Permitted (green): Tapped out, Omniscience on board → Cast Swan Song
    • Permitted (green): Tapped out, Treasonous Ogre on board → Cast Red Elemental Blast
    • Banned (red): Tapped out, Shimmer Myr on board → Flash Lotus Petal → Cast Swan Song
    • Banned (red): Tapped out → Exile Simian Spirit Guide → Cast Red Elemental Blast
  • Budget Cap — $500 Moxfield
  • No Games After Supper

Tribal Mode

The commander and all creatures in the deck must share the same creature type.
